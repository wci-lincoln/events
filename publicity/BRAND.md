# WCI Lincoln brand guide, and how the Shiloh kit applies it

The church's brand guideline (typography, tone of voice, photography) as supplied to the
Shiloh 2026 committee in November 2026, followed by how each rule is applied in this kit.

## Typography

| Font | Assigned use (guide) | In the Shiloh kit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bebas Neue | Sunday theme | Every display headline: "Shiloh 2026", "Far Above", the countdown numbers, "Today at Shiloh", "Scan me". |
| Bebas Kai | Sunday flyer: date, address, transport | Dates, times, addresses, pick-up points, session names, the lockup, tags and small labels. |
| Gontserrat | Mid-week date, address, social | Body text, captions, notes and scripture (set in italic). |
| Brother Signature | "Join us this Sunday" invitation line | One invitation line per piece: "A personal invitation", "I'll save you a seat", "Come expecting, and bring someone with you", "Clear the week", the word "days" in the number-in-words countdown. Never for times, scripture or anything longer than a sentence. |
| The Meziraine | Mid-week service theme | Reserved for the church's mid-week service designs. Not used in the Shiloh kit, so the campaign keeps one theme face. |

Website: Playfair Display for display headings and Source Sans 3 for body text and
navigation. The website fonts stay off the posters and the poster fonts stay off long-form
website text.

Type hierarchy for a service design: invitation line first (Brother Signature), then the
practical information (date, time, address, postcode in Bebas Kai) with enough size and
space to read quickly.

## Tone of voice

Scripture rooted, joyful and expectant. Write like a host welcoming someone at the door, not a
notice board.

| Voice | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Warm | "Join us for a glorious time in His presence." |
| Bold | "Liberating the world through the Word of Faith." |
| Practical | "Join us this Sunday. See the confirmed service time and venue below." |
| Testifying | "Tell of God's love and faithfulness in your life." |

Do: use "you" and "we"; lead with an invitation, then the time, place and postcode; use
familiar church terms accurately (Covenant Hour of Prayer, Shiloh, WSF, Word of Faith); UK
English; one time format, `9:20am`; restrained punctuation.

Avoid: generic wellness language ("holistic approach", "inner peace"); long all-capital
paragraphs. Capitals are for short display titles and concise labels.

In the kit: times are written `5:00am`, `11:30am`, `5:00pm` everywhere (cards, captions,
guides). Sentences are set in Gontserrat in sentence case; only titles and labels are in
capitals (Bebas has no lower case). Captions lead with the invitation and end with the time
and place.

## Photography

- Real church life: worship, laughter, families, teens and preaching. No generic stock imagery.
- Clear, well-lit photographs. Keep faces visible and do not place text across them.
- Where a photo carries text, a soft blush wash can support legibility (roughly 80 to 85%),
  only where the photograph still reads well.
- Speaker cut-outs: clean background, portrait facing the headline.
- Get consent before using children's photos.

In the kit: the one portrait is Bishop David Oyedepo, cut out on a crimson or gold ground and
facing the headline; no text sits on his face. No children appear.
