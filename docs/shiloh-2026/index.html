Before you come

What it is, when, where, arriving

What is Shiloh?Since 1999. Why the whole Winners family stops for it

Shiloh is the annual prophetic gathering of Living Faith Church Worldwide (Winners’ Chapel), instituted in 1999 by God through His servant Bishop David Oyedepo. Every December the Winners family gathers at Canaanland, Ota, and in viewing centres across the nations, to hear the Word, pray, and receive what God has for the coming year.

Come for all of it

The Word at Shiloh builds from the Prayer Hour through the Morning Session and the classes into the Encounter Night, so plan your week around all six days and come expecting.

    What happens when I arrive?Welcome desk, first-timers, what to bring

    Come to the welcome desk at the entrance if it is your first time. Someone will show you where to sit and where everything is.
