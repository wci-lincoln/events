Before you come

What it is, when, where, arriving

What is Shiloh?Since 1998. Why the whole Winners family stops for it

Shiloh is the annual prophetic gathering of Living Faith Church Worldwide (Winners’ Chapel), instituted in 1998 by God through His servant Bishop David Oyedepo. Every December the Winners family gathers at Canaanland, Ota, and in viewing centres across the nations, to hear the Word, pray, and receive what God has for the coming year.

Shiloh 2026 runs Tuesday 8 to Sunday 13 December under the theme Far Above.

Come for all of it

Six days, every session. The Word at Shiloh builds from the Prayer Hour through the Morning Session and the classes into the Encounter Night, and each day builds on the one before. Plan your week around it and come expecting.

Everyone is welcome: members, first-timers, friends and family. Think of one person who needs this and bring them along.

    What happens when I arrive?Welcome desk, first-timers, what to bring

    Come to the welcome desk at the entrance if it is your first time. Someone will show you where to sit and where everything is.

    Arrive before the session starts and stay to the end. Each session is one piece of the whole.

    What to bring

    Yourself first, and someone with you. Then your Bible, notebook and pen, a warm layer for the early-morning sessions, and your offering.
